# The Quest for the Golden Plaque - Interactive Video Engine Specification
**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** May 28, 2026  
**Created for:** Nic & Team (Symbiotic Human-AI Ascension Project)  
**Goal:** Build a fully functional, scalable "Choose-Your-Path" video adventure engine that teaches the Golden Rule through an engaging, emotional story while protecting kids from real-world digital traps.

## 1. Project Overview
This engine powers an interactive story series called **"The Quest for the Golden Plaque"** starring Dragon (stoic, witty teacher) and Panda (eager, impulsive learner).  
Panda sees a flashy online video promising a literal "Golden Ruler" that will make him richer than anyone — but it’s a scam requiring him to share private info or recruit friends. Dragon turns the hunt into a real adventure that reveals the **true treasure**: a beautiful golden plaque engraved with the Golden Rule.

**Core Message:**  
The plaque is worth all the gold in the world because living by “Do unto others as you would have others do unto you” creates lasting harmony, confidence, and success far beyond any material wealth.

The engine supports:
- Quick 30-second Shorts (Phase 1)
- Full interactive “Choose-Your-Path” adventures (Phase 2)
- Automatic consolidation into narrated storybook videos
- Hosting on our own monetizable “Harmony Quest Tube” platform
